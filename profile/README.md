# Nightworks AI

Nightworks AI prepares recurring finance work for controllers, CFOs and business owners, with source evidence and a person deciding every irreversible action.

**What it prepares:** month-end close preparation, account reconciliation, accounts payable, accounts receivable, marketplace settlement reconciliation (Amazon, Shopify), invoice and spend audit, cash forecasting, sales tax preparation, and audit preparation with evidence linked to source records.

**How it works:** agents prepare the work from the source systems, with evidence linked to source records. Permissions are recorded. Checks run against the source systems. A person reviews and decides every irreversible action: posting, paying, filing.

**Who it is for:** controllers, CFOs, owner-operators, CPA firms doing client accounting, and holdco and private equity operators.

**How to start:** a free proof of concept on one agreed finance workflow. Production scope and pricing are agreed separately.
